A forum post or message the owner sends as themselves is written the way a
person writes to colleagues: short plain paragraphs, an informal opening and
close, at most one short list. No bold labels, no stacked bullets, no formal
spec-citation style.

**Why:** the owner rejected structured, formal drafts as reading like AI.

**How to apply:** keep spec detail to what a reader needs and link the rest.
A post citing openEHR specifications links the development branch
(`specifications.openehr.org/releases/<component>/development/...`), which
the owner treats as the current text. Earlier Discourse threads are quoted
with `[quote="user, post:N, topic:T"]`. Related: the writing-style rule.
